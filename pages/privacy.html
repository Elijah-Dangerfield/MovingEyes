Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Moving Eyes is designed to collect as little information about you as possible. This page explains what is and isn't collected.

What's stored on your device

The app stores data locally on your phone, and nowhere else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

What's sent off your device

Two things, both about the app rather than about you.

Crash reports, sent to Sentry when the app crashes or hits an error, so the bug can be found and fixed. A report contains:

  • A crash log: what went wrong, and where in the code it happened.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • The version of the app you're running.

Usage and diagnostic events, sent to Grafana Cloud. These record how the app itself behaves: that a display session ran and for how long, that a scene was saved, that a screen was opened, and warnings or errors the app logged along the way.

Both carry two random identifiers: one for your installation of the app and one for the current run of it. They are generated by the app, they are not your advertising ID or any device serial number, and they are not linked to a name, email address or account. Deleting the app discards the installation identifier, and a fresh install generates a new one.

Neither of these ever contains a scene you built, a photo, or anything captured by the microphone.

What isn't collected

The app does not:

  • Show ads, or include any advertising SDK or ad network.
  • Use an advertising identifier.
  • Track your location.
  • Sell or share your data with anyone beyond the two services named above.
  • Require an account. There is no sign-in anywhere in the app.

Deleting your data

Everything you make in the app lives on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. To have the crash reports and usage events tied to your installation deleted, email contact@nightjarlabs.llc, or use Report a bug or Send feedback in the app, which includes your installation identifier so the records can be found.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone (optional): only if you switch on Sound reactivity, so the eyes can look toward doors, voices and footsteps. Audio is analysed on your device, moment to moment, and immediately discarded. Nothing is recorded, nothing is written to storage, and no audio or anything derived from it ever leaves your device. The app reads only how loud the room is and which microphone a sound reached first; it does not process speech and cannot recognise what is said. Declining costs you nothing else — every other feature works, and the permission is never asked for again unless you turn the setting on yourself.

That is the only permission the app asks for.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here and the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised.
